Salsa (Canned)

From Shelly, 8/2010 · simmer 2 hours · process 15 min · his uploaded recipe card

Salsa (Canned) from Shelly, 8/2010 — a simmer-and-jar batch.

Ingredients

  • 16–18 tomatoes (no skin)
  • 3 cups chopped peppers (any color)
  • 2 cups chopped onion
  • 5–6 jalapeño peppers (3 with seeds, 2 without)
  • 6 cloves garlic, chopped
  • 4 tsp salt
  • 10 tbsp sugar
  • 1 cup vinegar
  • 1½ tbsp pepper

Method

  1. Mix all together and simmer 2 hours.
  2. Put in jars and process 15 minutes.

Yield as written on the card: “Makes about 1 pint jars (the number is hard to read on the card).” Recipe exactly as written, 2026-11-13.
