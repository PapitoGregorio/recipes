Keto Granola Bars with Coconut

No-bake · Makes 8 bars · the coconut feature bar

The coconut feature bar — coconut inside, coconut oil as the binder, chocolate drizzle on top.

Ingredients

  • ½ cup natural peanut butter (or almond butter)
  • ¼ cup coconut oil, plus 1 tsp for the drizzle
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • ⅓ cup granulated sweetener, like erythritol
  • ½ cup sliced almonds, plus 1 extra Tbsp to decorate on top
  • ⅓ cup flaxseed meal
  • 1 Tbsp chia seeds
  • ⅓ cup pumpkin seeds
  • ¼ cup unsweetened desiccated coconut
  • ½ tsp ground cinnamon
  • 3 Tbsp sugar-free chocolate chips (for the drizzle)

Method

  1. Line a 9×5-inch loaf pan with parchment paper. Set aside.
  2. In a medium mixing bowl or a saucepan, place the wet ingredients: peanut butter, coconut oil, and vanilla. Microwave by 30-second bursts, stirring and repeating until the coconut oil is fully melted and combines with the nut butter — not more than 1 minute 30 seconds. Or melt in a saucepan over medium heat, stirring often.
  3. Stir in the sugar-free crystal sweetener and microwave an extra 30 seconds to incorporate well.
  4. In a large mixing bowl, add the dry ingredients: sliced almonds, flaxseed meal, chia seeds, pumpkin seeds, shredded coconut, and cinnamon. Stir to combine.
  5. Pour the nut butter mixture onto the dry ingredients and stir with a spatula until everything is evenly covered.
  6. Transfer the mixture to the prepared loaf pan. Press evenly with your hand to leave no air, and flatten the surface with a spatula.
  7. Freeze for 20 minutes, until the bars are hard and set. Lift out on the parchment and sprinkle the extra sliced almonds on top.
  8. Melt the sugar-free dark chocolate and coconut oil in a small bowl in the microwave. Drizzle over the top and return to the freezer for 1–3 minutes, until the chocolate is set.
  9. Cut into 8 bars. Wrap each bar individually in plastic wrap or bee wax.

Storage: wrap each bar individually and keep in the fridge for up to 8 days. The author's sweetener note: erythritol doesn't fully dissolve in no-bake goods — that little sweet crunch is expected.
