Pad Thai with Chicken

Pulmuone kit · stovetop · about 5 minutes

One pan, five minutes, straight off the package directions.

Ingredients

  • 1 Pulmuone Pad Thai with Chicken kit (2 rice noodle packs, 1 vegetables & chicken pack, 1 sauce pack, 1 peanut topping pack)

Method

  1. Both rice noodle packs into a frying pan — throw away the little freshness packet, don't eat it.
  2. Pour the sauce pack over the noodles, plus only the water from the vegetables & chicken pack.
  3. On medium heat, gently loosen the noodles for 2 minutes.
  4. Add the rest of the vegetables & chicken pack and stir-fry 1–2 minutes.
  5. Turn the heat off, add the peanut topping pack and mix well. Plate and enjoy.

Don't add the vegetables & chicken solids at the start — they go in after the noodles loosen, or they overcook.
