Keto Chocolate Protein Bars (Nut Free)

No-bake · Makes 16 bars · nut-free · 3 g net carbs each

Nut-free — sunflower seeds and seed butter with chocolate protein and cocoa, chilled and sliced.

Ingredients

  • 4 oz sunflower seeds, raw and unsalted
  • 4 oz sunflower seed butter (or tahini, or any nut butter if no nut allergies)
  • 2 scoops chocolate protein powder, or 75 g
  • 3 oz unsweetened cocoa powder
  • ¾ cup monk fruit allulose confectioners sweetener
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • 8 Tbsp butter, softened (or coconut oil)
  • Optional coating: ½ cup sugar-free dark chocolate chips and 1 Tbsp butter

Method

  1. Place all ingredients into a food processor and blend until smooth.
  2. Scrape down the sides of the processor and blend again, until it looks shiny and slightly wet.
  3. Taste and adjust sweetener if needed.
  4. Spread the batter into a parchment lined 8×4 inch loaf pan. Use the sides of the parchment over the top of the batter to press it down and flatten evenly without getting your hands dirty.
  5. Refrigerate for 30 minutes. Slice into 8 bars, then cut the bars in half to make 16 bars.
  6. If using the optional coating: freeze for 30 minutes first. Melt the chocolate chips and butter in the microwave for 1 minute or on the stove top and stir until smooth. Dip the bottom half of the bars into the melted chocolate, lay on a parchment lined baking sheet, and drizzle more chocolate on top if desired.

Per bar: 151 cal · 6 g protein · 5 g carbs · 3 g net carbs. Storage: no refrigeration needed; airtight on the counter up to 5 days, in the fridge up to 2 weeks, or frozen for a month. The author prefers a loaf pan over an 8×8 — the bars come out too thin in the square pan.
