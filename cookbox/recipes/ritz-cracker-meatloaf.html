Ritz Cracker Meatloaf

Serves 8 · Prep 10 min · Cook 1 hr at 375°F · his uploaded recipe card

His meatloaf, from the uploaded card. The glaze goes on in two rounds.

Ingredients

  • 2 lb ground beef (80–85% fat)
  • 1 cup crushed Ritz crackers
  • ¾ cup milk
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup yellow onion, diced
  • ½ cup red bell pepper
  • ½ cup green bell pepper
  • 1½ tbsp butter or oil
  • 1 tbsp minced garlic
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tbsp onion powder
  • 1 tbsp garlic powder
  • 1 tbsp dried parsley
  • 1½ tsp dried thyme
  • 1½ tsp dried basil
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tbsp salt
  • 1½ tsp pepper
  • — Glaze —
  • ¾ cup ketchup
  • ¾ cup sweet chili sauce
  • ¼ cup brown sugar

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F. Line a baking sheet with foil.
  2. Sauté bell peppers and onion in butter or oil until soft. Cool slightly.
  3. Soak crushed Ritz in milk for 5 minutes.
  4. Mix ketchup, chili sauce, and brown sugar. Save half for the top.
  5. In a large bowl, combine beef, cracker mix, vegetables, eggs, and all seasonings.
  6. Mix gently. Do not overmix. Shape into a loaf on the sheet.
  7. Spread half the glaze on top. Wrap loosely in foil. Bake 30 min.
  8. Add the rest of the glaze. Bake 30 more min, until 165°F inside.
  9. Rest 10 min before slicing.

Garlic and onion powder are 1 tbsp each — use 2 tbsp each for a bolder taste. Leftovers: fridge 3–4 days, freezer up to 3 months. Recipe exactly as written on the card he uploaded, 2026-11-13.
