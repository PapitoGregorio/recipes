Lemongrass Basil Chicken Stir Fry

His own wok dinner · from his photos, Nov 2025

His stir fry, written up from his step-by-step photos — chicken first, vegetables by heat order, sauce last.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb chicken breast, thin-sliced
  • 2–3 carrots, cut in small coins
  • 1 red onion, cut in chunks
  • 1 zucchini, half-moons
  • 1 yellow squash, half-moons
  • 2 cups broccoli florets
  • 2–3 heads bok choy, chopped (stems and leaves)
  • 1 can water chestnuts, drained
  • Avocado oil (Chosen Foods)
  • 1 packet Kevin's Lemongrass Basil Sauce
  • San-J tamari, a splash to taste

Method

  1. Cut everything first, like the photos. Stir fry moves too fast to cut as you go.
  2. Wok on high heat, add avocado oil. Cook the chicken until no longer pink, about 4–5 minutes. Take it out, set aside.
  3. Same wok, a little more oil: carrots and red onion first — 3 minutes. They're the slowest to soften.
  4. Add broccoli — 2 minutes.
  5. Add zucchini and yellow squash — 2 minutes, still a little firm.
  6. Add bok choy stems, then the leaves and the water chestnuts — 1 minute, just until the leaves wilt.
  7. Chicken back in. Pour the Lemongrass Basil sauce over, add a splash of tamari, toss everything 1–2 minutes until glossy and hot.
  8. Serve as-is, or over rice.

Quantities are a best read from his photos, not measured amounts.
